Orange Chicken


Description

This is a recipe for Chinese Orange Chicken that makes impressively crispy chicken pieces without deep frying. The sauce has great orange flavour, it’s not too sweet, and I love the background hint of ginger!


Ingredients

  • Chicken Thighs/Chicken Breast
  • Cornstarch
  • Black pepper
  • Soy sauce
  • Egg white
  • Water
  • Garlic & Ginger
  • Oranges
  • Brown Sugar
  • Rice wine vinegar
  • Sesame seed
  • Green onions

Steps

  1. Cut your chicken into bite-size pieces
  2. Marinate the chicken with egg whites, cornstarch, blackpepper, soy sauce, and water
  3. Mix until you get thin, paint-like batter consistency
  4. Fry the chickens until golden brown (around 4-5 minutes)
  5. (Optional) Double-fry the chickens for maximum crunchiness!
  6. Grate some garlic, ginger, and zest some oranges
  7. Mix the grated garlic, ginger, orange zest with soy sauce, fresh orange juice, brown sugar, and rice wine vinegar
  8. Bring the sauce to a boil, and add some cornstarch to thicken the sauce
  9. Toss the fried chicken pieces into the sauce, mix for a few minutes
  10. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions
