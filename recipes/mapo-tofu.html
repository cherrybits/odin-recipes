Mapo Tofu


Description

This mapo tofu recipe (麻婆豆腐) is the true blue, authentic real deal—the spicy, tongue-numbing, rice-is-absolutely-not-optional, can’t-have-just-one-scoop mapo tofu that you get in the restaurants.


Ingredients

  • Tofu
  • Ground Beef/Pork
  • Chili Bean Paste (Doubanjiang)
  • Sichuan peppercorns oil
  • Garlic, Ginger, Scallions
  • Soy sauce, Sugar, White pepper
  • Shaoxing Wine
  • Sesame Oil
  • Stock
  • White Rice (optional)

Steps

  1. Mince garlic, ginger, and slice the white parts of scallions
  2. Cut the tofu into cubes
  3. Sautee the ground beef (or pork) until the water evaporates
  4. Add the scallions, ginger, and garlic, along with Doubanjiang and Sichuan peppercorn oil
  5. Cook for a couple of minutes
  6. Once fragrant, add shaoxing wine
  7. Then, season with soy sauce, sugar, and white pepper
  8. Give it a mix before adding in stock, then add the tofu
  9. Bring it to a simmer, then add cornstarch slurry, combine it until it thickens
  10. Finish with a little sesame oil
  11. Serve with rice! (optional)
